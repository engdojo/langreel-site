Account Deletion

Use this page to request deletion of your LangReel account and associated data.

If you can access the app

Open Profile in LangReel and choose Delete Account. After confirmation, your account and associated cloud data controlled by LangReel will be deleted.

If you cannot access the app

For identity verification, send the request from the email address registered with LangReel.

Send deletion request

We normally complete verified requests within 30 days. We may ask for additional information when necessary to verify the account.

Data that will be deleted

  • Your LangReel authentication account
  • Account information controlled by LangReel, such as email address, display name, and user identifier
  • LangReel cloud data associated with the account

On-device data and retained information

Reset the app or uninstall it to remove learning history, saved phrases, and settings stored on your device. Minimal records required for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, or security purposes, and purchase records managed by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat, may remain according to their respective retention policies.

Subscriptions

Deleting your LangReel account does not automatically cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately through the subscription settings of the store where you purchased it.
